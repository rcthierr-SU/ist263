Spider-Man

The original cinematic run of Spider-Man ran from 2002 until 2007. It spanned 3 movies and starred Toby Maguire as the titular Spider-Man. All three movies were directed by Sam Raimi.

Quick Facts:

  • Movie Names: Spider-Man, Spider-Man 2, Spider-Man 3
  • Runtimes: 121 minutes, 127 minutes, 139 minutes
  • Director: Sam Raimi
  • Main Actor: Toby Maguire
  • Villains: Green Goblin, Doc Ock, Venom/Sandman


Commentary: These movies are classic. They are the reason that I, and so many others, love Spider-Man. I remember the first time I watched Spider-Man (2002) when I was maybe 6 and I have never been the same. That being said, on the rewatch, there are some flaws in these movies. They feel like they focus more on the Spider-Man part but the characterization of Peter Parker falls very flat at times.
