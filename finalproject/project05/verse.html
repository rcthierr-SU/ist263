Spider-Verse

The last Spider-Man Series being discussed is the only animated franchise, the Spider-Verse series. So far, this series comprises two movies with a third to be released in June 2027. Other than the main difference of being animated compared to live-action, the Spider-Verse movies follow Miles Morales. A 15 year old who takes up the mantle of Spider-Man following the death of Peter Parker.

Quick Facts:

  • Movie Names: Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse, Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse
  • Runtimes: 116 minutes, 140 minutes
  • Director: Peter Ramsey/Bob Persichetti/Rodney Rothman, Joaquim Dos Santos/Kemp Powers/Justin K. Thompson
  • Main Actor: Tom Holland
  • Villains: Kingpin/Doc Ock, Spot/ Miguel O'Hara


Commentary:
Honestly, the animated series is one of my favorite Spider-man movies. Into the Spider-Verse fundamentally changed pre-teen Ayrn as a person in the best way ever. These movies gave me a new found respect for animation and I loved seeing a person of color as my favorite superhero on the big screen (further proof that representation matters). The main reason I love these movies is the visuals. These movies are STUNNING! Into the Spider-Verse has a scene called “The Leap of Faith” and genuinely it might be my favorite piece of cinema ever! I will die on this hill.
