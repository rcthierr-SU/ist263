MCU Spider-Man

The run of Spider-Man is simply titled just that, but the character has been incorporated into the Marvel Cinematic Universe which is owned by Disney. Previously the rights to the Spider-Man IP had been owned by Sony. The Spider-Man character, played by Tom Holland, was first introduced in Captain America: Civil War (2016, The Russo Brothers) and has been in 7 movies. The MCU version has gotten the most title films with the most recent movie being released in July 2026. The first three movies were directed by Jon Watts and the fourth was directed by Destin Daniel Cretton.

Quick Facts:

  • Movie Names: Spider-Man: Homecoming, Spider-Man: Far From Home, Spider-Man: No Way Home, Spider-Man: Brand New Day
  • Runtimes: 133 minutes, 130 minutes, 148 minutes, 145 minutes
  • Director: Jon Watts/Destin Daniel Cretton
  • Main Actor: Tom Holland
  • Villains: Vulture, Mysterio, Green Goblin/Doc Ock/Electro/Lizard/Sandman, Jean Grey/The (literal) Government

Commentary:
Of the live-action runs of Spider-Man, the MCU version is definitely my favorite. This is because when those movies were released I was around the age that Peter Parker was supposed to be. This allowed me to see myself more in the character. This was especially true with the most recent movie where Peter is a broke 20-something living in New York trying to figure himself out. Something I can heavily relate to. Sure I’m not fighting a ginger with psycho powers or the literal government, but I know how it feels to not know my place in the world or what the hell I’m doing with my life. One of the main qualms I have with these films is that in the first 2 movies Peter is stuck cleaning up Tony Stark’s messes instead of fighting his own villains, and the third movie is pretty much fan service AND they kill Aunt May. It was good fan service, but fan service nonetheless.
